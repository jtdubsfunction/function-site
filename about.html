About

John Winterkorn

John founded Function after more than fifteen years in marketing, most of them in brand strategy. His most recent role was leading brand strategy across Church & Dwight's portfolio at Razorfish.

From 2018 to 2026 John was at Razorfish, where he rose from Director of Strategy to Group Vice President and led brand across Church & Dwight's portfolio of 12 brands and business units. Over that time the portfolio grew sales by more than $1 billion. After tripling the size of the account, he was promoted to one of its primary leaders and pulled the agency's disciplines into a single team that could take on work from brand platforms to DTC growth strategy. He worked closely with almost 100 people on the client side, from the CMO to junior brand managers.

Before that he was a Strategy Director at 360i, working for Nestlé Waters, Nespresso, MetLife, and AB-InBev, and at McCann, where he defined a global engagement strategy for polio eradication with UNICEF, worked on a global content strategy refresh of Mastercard's Priceless platform, and did exploratory positioning work for USPS. At Rokkan, his work for American Express Serve was shortlisted at Cannes Lions in 2014, and he wrote the strategy and brief for Anthem's first digital consumer campaign.

His first job was in tax strategy for healthcare firms (it turns out death and taxes are a good countercyclical play for a first job during a widening financial meltdown). He studied Government and Legal Studies at Bowdoin, with a minor in Japanese History and Literature.
